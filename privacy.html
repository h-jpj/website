Privacy Policy

My Allotment Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Summary

My Allotment does not collect, sell, share, or transmit personal data to Portas Productions.

The app is designed to work offline. Your allotment data stays on your device unless you choose to move it yourself using your device’s own backup or sharing tools.

Who provides the app

My Allotment is provided by Portas Productions.

Contact: jay@portasproductions.co.uk

Information stored in the app

When you use My Allotment, you may choose to enter or add:

  • plot and bed layouts
  • crop and planting records
  • tasks, notes, and journal entries
  • photos you choose to attach to your journal

This information is stored locally on your device. Portas Productions does not receive or access it.

Data collection, sharing, and tracking

  • No account is required.
  • No personal data is sent to Portas Productions.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs are used.
  • No app data is sold or shared with third parties.

Photos and device permissions

If you choose to add photos, the app may ask for permission to access your camera or photo library. This is only used so you can add images to your own garden journal.

Photos remain on your device and are not uploaded to Portas Productions.

Deleting your data

You can delete your own plots, plantings, notes, tasks, and photos inside the app where those options are available. You can also remove app data by uninstalling the app or clearing its local data through your device settings.

Children’s privacy

My Allotment is not directed at children. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children or anyone else.

Changes to this policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
